Session 1 · The second buyer

What the agent can't verify.

An agent doesn't trust your support copy or your adjectives. It trusts what it can check — certifications, authority, structured data. Most listings give it nothing.

// the field

92% score zero

Across ~600 listings, 92% expose no machine-verifiable trust signal an agent can confirm without a human in the loop.

// the 8%

The few that win

Under 1 in 12 carry verifiable certs and structured proof. To an agent shortlisting vendors, they're the only credible options on the page.

// the move

Become checkable

Surface SOC 2 / HIPAA / ISO, domain authority, and FAQ schema as data — not prose. It's the cheapest agent-trust win on the board.
